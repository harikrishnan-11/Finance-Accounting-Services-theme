Our story

We started Stackly to make finance feel simple

Stackly began in 2012 with two accountants and one belief: business owners shouldn’t need a finance degree to understand their own numbers.

Today, more than 120 certified accountants, tax specialists and analysts help over 5,000 businesses stay compliant, cut costs and plan with confidence.

  • Certified experts, assigned to your business
  • Modern cloud tools, clear monthly reporting
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
